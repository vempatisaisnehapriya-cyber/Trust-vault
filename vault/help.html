TrustVault Help Center

Welcome! Follow these steps to use TrustVault.

1. Upload Documents

Enter a document name, select your file and click Save to my vault.

2. Search Documents

Enter the document name in the search box and click Search.

3. Download Documents

Click Download to save your document to your device.

4. Delete Documents

Click Delete to remove a document from your vault.

5. Account

Use My Profile to view your profile and Logout to sign out.
